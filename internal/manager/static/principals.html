Principals and leases

Every client of the volume logs in to the RESP proxy as a named principal. Macs enroll here and stay pending until you approve them. Approving or revoking rewrites the proxy's principals file and asks the proxy to reload it.

Enforcement mode

Passthrough: authenticate only. Observe: decide every write, refuse nothing, log what would be refused. Enforce: refuse writes into a project another principal holds. Step back to observe or passthrough at any time.

Orphan storage keys

minio-keyd reports these per-Mac storage keys, but this Manager's records do not know them (the Manager's store was lost or restored from an older copy, or a key was made by hand). Each one still works at the object store. They are kept until you remove them. Remove them only when no Mac still needs one: a Mac that uses one loses object-store access until it has its own key.
