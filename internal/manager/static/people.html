People and access

Give each Mac to the person who uses it. A person with access all writes everywhere. The first folder you grant limits that person to granted folders: a client folder (/Clients, every project in it, and creating projects there) or one project (/Clients/A). From then on grants are in force: a Mac that belongs to no person (a new Mac, or the Macs of a removed person) can read but not write until you assign it. Refused writes fail on the Mac as a read-only error. Reading is not limited.
